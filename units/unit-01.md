### 1.1 Network Devices and Engineering Context

The V2 unit also introduces the development of communication networks, enterprise versus carrier networks, network-device roles, and the work of network engineers. It distinguishes modular devices, whose replaceable boards support larger core deployments, from fixed devices whose functions are integrated into a chassis and are commonly used in access or smaller deployments. These are course examples and placement patterns, not universal product-selection rules. The unit closes by connecting network-engineering work to planning, construction, maintenance, troubleshooting, and optimization.
